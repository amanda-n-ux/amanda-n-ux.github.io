Adding Agents to Intune

Agents as a Managed System

Problem
Intune’s first agents, with no precedent — and admins had to trust an agent before they’d let it act for them.
My part
Experience strategy and interaction design for the shared framework every Intune agent is built on.
Result
Multiple agents shipped to public preview together, on schedule for Ignite.

02
VRA Deep Dive

Vulnerability Remediation Agent

Problem
One CVE could touch thousands of devices, and fixing it meant stitching Defender and Intune together by hand.
My part
Lead UX, end to end — discoverability, setup, ranked suggestions, the approval workflow, and tracking.
Result
One guided, admin-reviewed remediation flow inside Intune. For one early customer, roughly two weeks of remediation work dropped to under two minutes.

Two related cases · start anywhere

Complete lifecycle ownership — taking a brand-new product's admin experience from pre-launch through general availability and ongoing management, building on earlier Azure Virtual Desktop experience.

01
Complete Lifecycle Ownership

Provisioning Cloud PCs at Scale

Problem
A per-user license had to become something admins could deploy to thousands of devices at once.
My part
Design lead — a group-based provisioning model and a device state model Intune had never needed.
Result
Provisioning built to scale to thousands of Cloud PCs at a time, not one at a time.
